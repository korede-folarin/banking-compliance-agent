VULNERABLE CUSTOMER ESCALATION PROCEDURE
Northbridge Consumer Lending — Internal Procedure
Version 2.1 — Internal use only (Customer Service, Underwriting, and Risk
teams)

1. PURPOSE
1.1 This procedure sets out when staff must proactively escalate a case
    for vulnerability review, in addition to (not instead of) responding
    to any vulnerability a customer discloses directly.

2. PROACTIVE ESCALATION TRIGGERS
2.1 Staff must escalate a case to the Specialist Support Team, even
    where the customer has not self-disclosed a vulnerability, where any
    of the following is present on the account:
    (a) two or more missed payment flags in the trailing 12 months;
    (b) an open financial hardship marker of any kind;
    (c) account notes indicating a recent bereavement, relationship
        breakdown, or significant health event.
2.2 Escalation must happen before, not after, any new lending decision
    is finalised for that customer.

3. ESCALATION PROCESS
3.1 Complete the Specialist Support referral form, referencing the
    specific trigger(s) from section 2.1.
3.2 The Specialist Support Team will respond within 2 working days with
    a recommended support plan.
3.3 Underwriting must not proceed to final approval until the
    Specialist Support Team has either confirmed no further action is
    needed, or the recommended support plan has been actioned.

4. RECORD-KEEPING
4.1 All escalations and outcomes must be logged on the customer's
    account record for audit purposes.
