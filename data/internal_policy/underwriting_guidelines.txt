INTERNAL UNDERWRITING GUIDELINES
Northbridge Consumer Lending — Retail Credit Underwriting Standards
Version 4.2 — Internal use only (Underwriting, Risk, and Compliance teams)

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 These guidelines set the minimum internal standard for approving
    retail consumer loan agreements before they are issued to a
    customer. They are stricter, in places, than the minimum required
    by external FCA regulation, and underwriters must apply whichever
    standard is more protective of the customer.

2. FEE AND CHARGE DISCLOSURE (INTERNAL STANDARD)
2.1 Every fee or charge that may be payable under a loan agreement must
    be stated as a specific monetary amount directly within the
    agreement itself, before the agreement is presented to the customer
    for signature.
2.2 A clause that refers the customer to a separate "tariff of
    charges," "schedule of fees," or similar document available "on
    request" does not satisfy this standard, even where external
    guidance might arguably tolerate it as one factor among several.
    Internal policy treats an unquantified fee reference as a
    disclosure defect requiring remediation before approval.
2.3 Where clause 2.2 is breached, the agreement must be referred to an
    Underwriting Manager for review and may not proceed to signature
    until either (a) the fee is quantified in the agreement, or (b) the
    Underwriting Manager records a documented exception with reasoning.

3. TARGET MARKET AND AFFORDABILITY
3.1 Underwriters must confirm the product's stated target market
    assessment is present and that the applicant's circumstances, as
    known, are consistent with it.
3.2 Affordability must be assessed using the applicant's most recent 3
    months of account activity where available (see Retail Systems for
    account lookup access).

4. ESCALATION
4.1 Any case meeting one or more criteria in the Vulnerable Customer
    Escalation Procedure must be escalated in accordance with that
    procedure before underwriting sign-off.
