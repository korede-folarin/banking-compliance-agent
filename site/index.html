Banking
compliance agent

An AI that reviews loan agreements against the FCA Consumer Duty, cites the regulation behind every finding and hands anything uncertain to a person. Below: what it found in 34 test agreements, how reliable it was, and a step-by-step replay of real reviews.

This page is static and always on. The interactive review app runs on Streamlit and may take about 30 seconds to wake if nobody has used it recently.

What it found, and how reliable it was

Findings by Consumer Duty outcome

Each agreement counted once (its first review), as the reviewer receives it.

What the four outcomes mean
Price and value
Is the price fair for what the customer gets? Fees and charges must be justified.
Consumer support
Can customers get help when they need it, especially vulnerable customers?
Products and services
Is the product designed for, and sold to, the right customers?
Consumer understanding
Are the key terms explained clearly enough for the customer to understand?

    03 · Be the reviewer
    
    
    Every case goes to a person
    
    
    Open the review queue in the interactive app. Each verdict shows its reasoning, the quoted claims and the full FCA
      passage. Confirm it or override it. An override needs a written reason, and nothing resolves until every item is
      decided. Those rules are enforced in code.
