**Question.** If a `compliant` verdict skipped review, how often would it be a missed issue (label non_compliant)? A category qualifies for a risk target only if the exact 95% upper bound on that rate is at or below the target. One document counts once, however many runs it has.

- **Target 1%:** no category qualifies under any gate.
- **Target 2%:** no category qualifies under any gate.

The reason is the amount of evidence, not the errors seen. Even if a gate approved all 15 documents with zero missed issues, the bound would be 18.1%. To show a rate at or below a target with zero missed issues, a gate must approve at least:

| Risk target | Approved documents needed (0 missed) |

Those documents must be new ones, labelled before the run, with the gate fixed in advance (calibrate on the design split, then test once on held-out data).
